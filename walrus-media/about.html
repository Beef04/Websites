The story

Why I started a
content marketing agency.

It started with a camera and a fascination with how light, movement, and story can completely change the way you feel about a brand. I'd been filming for years — surf trips, car meets, coastal adventures — when I realised most brands were sitting on an untapped opportunity: authentic, cinematic content that didn't look like an ad and actually made people care.

Walrus Media was built to close that gap. We combine content strategy with world-class production — so every piece of content we make has a reason to exist and a job to do. Whether that's driving awareness, building trust, or converting a follower into a customer.

I'm involved from strategy through to final delivery. That means your content doesn't just look great — it's built with intent, consistency, and a clear understanding of what your brand needs to grow.
